## The Three Phases of the Retrograde (Brennan / George framework)

**Phase 1 — Evening Star Descending (shadow entry → station Rx, 31 Aug – 3 Oct).**
Venus is still direct but has passed the point she will eventually return to on her way out. Traditionally: the material of the cycle first surfaces, but often *unrecognized as such*. **Phase 2 — Underworld Descent (station Rx → cazimi, 3 Oct – 24 Oct).**
Venus sinks toward the Sun and is "burnt up" — traditionally read as Venus dying, going into the underworld, being reborn. The strongest interpretive tradition (Brennan citing Hellenistic sources; also modern Rick Levine) reads this as a period *not for launching Venus-ruled things* — not for signing partnership contracts, not for major aesthetic changes, not for beginning new relationships, not for concluding negotiations that require Venusian clarity. It *is* a period for review, for grieving what needs to be grieved, for the honest audit of what a relationship or an aesthetic project has actually become.

For Donnie, this phase is heavier than average because his LoY (Mars) rules the sign Venus is retrograding through. The year's amplifier and the retrograding planet are in dialogue the whole time. **This is a serious review period, not a light one.**

**Phase 3 — Underworld Return (cazimi → station Direct → shadow exit, 24 Oct – 15 Dec).**
Venus emerges as Morning Star, reborn, moving toward direct motion. The cazimi itself (24 Oct, 22:44 CDT) is the *turning point* — the moment Venus is closest to the Sun, "in the heart of the king." In the Hellenistic tradition, cazimi is anomalously a **strengthening** — the planet, though invisibly close to the Sun, is briefly at its most solar-authorized. Interpretations of the seed-moment of the new Venus synodic cycle. For Donnie the cazimi falls at **0°Scorpio 45' in H7** — the seed of a new Venus cycle in the house of the other. **This is the pivot of the whole cycle for him.**

Post-cazimi through station Direct: Venus is moving retrograde but has crossed the Sun; the underworld work is done, integration begins. Station Direct (13 Nov, 18:27 CST) is when the new understanding starts to take practical form.

Post-Direct through shadow exit: Venus re-covers ground she covered while retrograde, but now with the integration in place. The last two weeks of November and first half of December is when the *practical implementation* of whatever was reviewed becomes possible.

**Post-shadow tail (15 Dec – 30 Dec):** Venus passes back over the station-Rx degree and continues forward, passing his natal DSC on 30 Dec. **The retrograde's material lands definitively at his DSC around the end of December** — this is when whatever was reviewed about "the one across the table" becomes visible in a new form.
