# Aspect Guide — Plain-Language Interpretation

**Source:** `reference/aspect-guide-plain-language-v3.pdf` (Astrological Aspects & Planet-Pair Synthesis Guide V3: Plain-Language Interpretation Playbook)
**Filed:** 2026-08-16 by Urania at Austin's direction
**Purpose:** metaphor and phrasing library for the "so that means" layer of the three-part output shape when reading for a client with no astrological background. See MEMORY.md → *Reference Materials* for when to reach for it.

**Register discipline (Urania's note):** this is a *modern psychological / archetypal* frame, not a Hellenistic one. It does not encode sect, dignity, house topic, or timelord amplification. Use it as the plain-English translation layer over a Hellenistic-framed reading, not as a substitute for the frame. When the document's flat two-body read conflicts with what sect + dignity + house are actually doing in the subject's chart, the fuller read wins — this library provides *language*, not *judgment*.

**Aspects that cannot occur natally** (kept here so I do not offer a client a reading that is astronomically impossible):

- **Sun & Mercury:** only conjunction. Mercury never exceeds ~28° from the Sun.
- **Sun & Venus:** only conjunction. Venus never exceeds ~48° from the Sun.
- **Mercury & Venus:** only conjunction and sextile. They never separate by more than ~76°.

(These constraints do not apply to *transiting* aspects between the same pairs — the transiting body can be anywhere.)

## 1. Aspect metaphors (the five primary Ptolemaic aspects)

| Aspect | Angle | Beginner-friendly metaphor | What it feels like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conjunction** | 0° | *Two roommates sharing one mic* (fused energy) | The two parts are permanently blended — whenever one speaks, the other chimes in immediately. |
| **Sextile** | 60° | *An open doorway and a handy toolkit* (opportunity) | Two friendly parts that work well together *when consciously invited* to collaborate. |
| **Square** | 90° | *Internal friction and growth catalyst* (tension) | Two conflicting desires tugging at you; friction that forces discipline, problem-solving, and strength. |
| **Trine** | 120° | *Green light and natural highway* (natural flow) | Natural talent and ease. Things flow smoothly between these areas without overthinking. |
| **Opposition** | 180° | *A see-saw and a mirror* (relational polarization and balance) | Two opposing sides across a table — often one is owned in the self, the other projected onto partners until balance is learned. |

## 2. Planet archetypes (plain-English one-liners)

- **Sun** — core identity, conscious purpose, vitality, personal drive.
- **Moon** — emotional needs, instinctual comfort, gut reactions, inner vulnerability.
- **Mercury** — thinking style, communication, learning habits, daily problem-solving.
- **Venus** — values, relationship style, connection, aesthetics, what brings pleasure and peace.
- **Mars** — action engine, physical energy, boundaries, ambition, how anger/assertiveness is expressed.
- **Jupiter** — growth, optimism, perspective, worldview, learning curve, faith in life.
- **Saturn** — boundaries, maturity, responsibility, self-discipline, time, inner critic.
- **Uranus** — breakthrough, innovation, sudden change, individuation.
- **Neptune** — dreams, intuition, idealism, dissolution.
- **Pluto** — deep psychological transformation, power, buried material surfacing.

## 3. Delivery framework (three rules for client-facing reads)

1. **Translate the two roles.** Name the two parts of the person's life. *"Your emotional safety needs [Moon] and your drive to get things done [Mars]."*
2. **Describe the dynamic.** Explain the interaction using the aspect metaphor. *"They are in an opposition — two sides on a see-saw."*
3. **Give a practical takeaway.** Close with a concrete behavioral suggestion, not abstract theory. *"When you feel torn between holding back and reacting, pausing to name what you need keeps both sides balanced."*

**Urania's note:** This maps cleanly onto the three-part shape from SOUL.md (*the astrological event / located in your chart / so that means*). For a client with zero background, step 2 above collapses "the astrological event" and "located in your chart" into one sentence, and step 3 replaces modal-tradition language with a concrete behavioral suggestion. Sect and house topic still get named in the "located in" clause, but in plain English — *"your ninth house, which is where you form your worldview and long-term learning,"* not *"your ninth whole-sign, ruled by Jupiter, cadent."*

## 4. Planet-pair synthesis library

Format: **Aspect** — reading. Each pair below covers only the aspects that can natally occur (see restrictions at top). Transiting-aspect readings can borrow the same language freely.

### Sun & Moon — core identity & emotional self

- **Conjunction (New Moon):** What you want and how you feel are one and the same. Singular, focused sense of purpose. May struggle to see situations objectively from another's emotional perspective.
- **Sextile:** Head and heart communicate easily. With a little intentional reflection, personal goals align with emotional well-being without internal drama.
- **Square (Quarter Moon):** Internal tug-of-war between what you feel you "should" do and what you emotionally need to feel safe. Resolving this builds deep emotional resilience and drive.
- **Trine:** Deep inner peace and natural self-confidence. Conscious aspirations and emotional instincts naturally support each other.
- **Opposition (Full Moon):** Awareness through polarity. Pulled between self-expression and emotional connection to others. Finding balance creates an objective, self-aware individual who understands multiple viewpoints.

### Sun & Mercury — identity & mind

- **Conjunction:** Identity is deeply tied to how you think and express ideas. Direct personal authority in speech; may find it hard to detach ego from opinions.
- *(Only conjunction occurs natally.)*

### Sun & Venus — identity & connection/values

- **Conjunction:** Natural warmth, charm, innate desire for harmony and beauty. Wants to be liked; brings inviting, pleasant energy into any room.
- *(Only conjunction occurs natally.)*

### Sun & Mars — identity & drive/action

- **Conjunction:** Dynamic, high-octane energy, direct assertiveness. Takes immediate action; leads naturally. Can be impatient when things move too slowly.
- **Sextile:** Healthy, accessible engine for getting things done. When a goal is set, physical energy and focus arrive easily.
- **Square:** Restless drive, urge to prove yourself. May clash with authority or feel reactive; channeling the fire creates unstoppable achievement.
- **Trine:** Effortless courage and steady physical stamina. Pursues desires without forcing them; leadership feels natural and unforced.
- **Opposition:** Dynamic external confrontations or competitive encounters. Strong-willed others teach you to stand up for yourself with mature boundaries rather than defensive anger.

### Sun & Jupiter — identity & growth/optimism

- **Conjunction:** Generous, big-picture optimism, broad horizons. Inspires confidence; guard against overpromising or overlooking details.
- **Sextile:** Growth, travel, or learning opportunities show up often. Open-minded curiosity opens doors for personal advancement.
- **Square:** Big ambitions can lead to biting off more than you can chew. Balancing grand visions with realistic pacing creates extraordinary long-term success.
- **Trine:** Natural good humor, resilience, philosophical outlook. Faith that things work out often attracts positive outcomes.
- **Opposition:** High hopes and grand visions require a reality check from partnerships. Oscillates between optimism and feeling overextended; anchoring dreams in practical commitments.

### Sun & Saturn — identity & responsibility/discipline

- **Conjunction:** Serious, reliable, deeply accountable. Takes life seriously from an early age; builds immense credibility and lasting authority over time.
- **Sextile:** Practical self-discipline available when needed. Reliable; organizes daily efforts into concrete step-by-step progress.
- **Square:** Feels like you constantly have to work twice as hard to earn respect or self-worth. Overcoming the inner critic builds bedrock confidence and unmatched mastery.
- **Trine:** Natural patience, maturity, grounded endurance. Paces well; earns steady respect and stability without burning out.
- **Opposition:** Navigating heavy external expectations, demanding mentors, or rigid systems. Balancing personal identity with external responsibilities builds genuine self-respect without needing external validation.

### Moon & Mercury — feelings & communication

- **Conjunction:** Emotions and thoughts deeply intertwined. Expresses feelings with vivid detail; may overthink gut instincts.
- **Sextile:** Great emotional intelligence. Finds the right words for feelings; listens attentively without being overwhelmed.
- **Square:** Recurring split between logical reasoning and emotional instinct. Works through this into remarkable mediation and empathetic problem-solving.
- **Trine:** Intuitive communication flows naturally. Reads between lines, senses room mood, communicates with gentle clarity.
- **Opposition:** Debate between rational logic and deep emotional intuition. Intellectualizes emotions or attracts people who challenge feelings with facts; harmonizing both makes an extraordinarily balanced communicator.

### Moon & Venus — feelings & relationships/pleasure

- **Conjunction:** Deeply affectionate, nurturing, sensitive to beauty and comfort. Values peaceful environments; cares that others feel safe and loved.
- **Sextile:** Easy social grace and emotional generosity. Builds cozy, supportive friendships; navigates social settings smoothly.
- **Square:** Balancing personal emotional comfort with the desire to please partners or friends. Learning that boundaries don't break relationships is the key breakthrough.
- **Trine:** Natural warmth, charm, emotional sweetness. Relationships feel comforting; innate gift for welcoming, beautiful spaces.
- **Opposition:** Navigating differences between what feels emotionally safe (Moon) and what feels socially or romantically exciting (Venus). Integrating both creates fulfilling, secure, affectionate partnerships without sacrificing peace.

### Moon & Mars — feelings & action/reactivity

- **Conjunction:** Passionate, gut-level emotional honesty and quick emotional reactions. Defends loved ones fiercely; brings intense emotional vitality.
- **Sextile:** Constructive emotional drive. Channels feelings directly into positive physical action, creative projects, or advocacy.
- **Square:** Inner emotional volatility or irritability when needs aren't met instantly. Channeling into sports, creative outlets, or healthy boundary-setting creates fierce courage.
- **Trine:** Emotional confidence and healthy assertiveness. Knows how you feel and isn't afraid to act on it with directness and warmth.
- **Opposition:** Push-pull in relationships between self-protection and aggressive pursuit. Interpersonal friction teaches voicing emotional needs clearly before frustration boils over.

### Moon & Jupiter — feelings & optimism/nourishment

- **Conjunction:** Warm, generous, emotionally expansive. Loves taking care of people; may overextend or minimize real emotional discomfort with cheerfulness.
- **Sextile:** Easy emotional resilience and willingness to see the bright side. Finds generous allies when seeking support.
- **Square:** Big emotional highs and lows, or a tendency to soothe discomfort through overindulgence. Balanced routines give the expansive heart a steady home.
- **Trine:** Deep inner emotional safety and generosity. Naturally lifts spirits; bounces back from setbacks with grace.
- **Opposition:** Big swings between optimism and feeling overwhelmed by commitments. Setting healthy emotional boundaries keeps natural generosity sustainable.

### Moon & Saturn — feelings & boundaries/structure

- **Conjunction:** Emotionally self-reliant, reserved, cautious with the heart. Provides solid, unshakeable support for others; needs time to let down own walls.
- **Sextile:** Practical emotional maturity. Organizes life, sets healthy boundaries, stays composed during crises without shutting down empathy.
- **Square:** Early feeling of having to grow up fast or hold back vulnerability. Giving oneself permission to rest and be seen builds profound emotional wisdom.
- **Trine:** Calm emotional stability and patience. Not easily rattled; serves as a grounding anchor for everyone around.
- **Opposition:** Tug-of-war between emotional warmth and rigid self-protection. Relationships mirror where you hold back; opening up safely builds deeply dependable bonds.

### Mercury & Venus — communication & charm/harmony

- **Conjunction:** Diplomatic, articulate, charming conversationalist. Natural aesthetic eye; writes or speaks pleasantly; excels at putting people at ease.
- **Sextile:** Creative collaboration and easy social networking. Articulates ideas politely; builds bridges between differing viewpoints.
- *(Squares, trines, oppositions do not occur natally.)*

### Mercury & Mars — mind & drive/assertiveness

- **Conjunction:** Fast, razor-sharp intellect and direct speech. Straight to the point; thinks on feet; can be blunt or argumentative.
- **Sextile:** Decisive mental clarity. Articulates points assertively; turns plans into immediate physical execution without hesitation.
- **Square:** Restless mental energy; tendency to speak faster than think. Disciplined, becomes an exceptional debater, problem-solver, crisis strategist.
- **Trine:** Crisp, clear, persuasive communication. Confident without aggression; wins people over with logic and enthusiasm.
- **Opposition:** Mental sparring and verbal debates in relationships. Pushed to sharpen arguments and respect other viewpoints without turning conversations into competitive battles.

### Mercury & Jupiter — mind & big-picture vision

- **Conjunction:** Visionary thinking, love of learning, philosophical curiosity. Grasps big concepts easily; must occasionally double-check small logistical details.
- **Sextile:** Broad perspective and curiosity for new skills. Absorbs information easily; explains complex ideas in uplifting, accessible ways.
- **Square:** Overthinking grand plans while skipping practical steps. Balancing bold ideas with practical follow-through makes vision real.
- **Trine:** Natural storytelling gift, wisdom, encouraging mental outlook. Inspires others through teaching, writing, constructive advice.
- **Opposition:** Balancing fine details (Mercury) with broad vision (Jupiter). Debates between practical specifics and overarching philosophy develop incredible talent for translating high-level theory into practical reality.

### Mercury & Saturn — mind & structure/focus

- **Conjunction:** Exacting, disciplined, thorough thinking. Doesn't speak carelessly; ideas are well-researched, realistic, built to last.
- **Sextile:** Great organizational skills and attention to detail. Systematically breaks complicated problems into step-by-step solutions.
- **Square:** Tendency toward mental self-doubt or anxiety about mistakes. Pushing through creates incredible intellectual rigor, mastery, precision.
- **Trine:** Clear, methodical, calm intellect. Sound judgments; communicates with natural authority and common sense.
- **Opposition:** Intellectual skepticism or confronting rigid rules and critical feedback from others. Learning that constructive criticism sharpens thinking builds unshakeable mental clarity.

### Venus & Mars — connection & passion/drive

- **Conjunction:** Magnetic personal charm, passionate creativity, vibrant romantic energy. Actively pursues what is desired; charisma in social dynamics.
- **Sextile:** Smooth balance between giving and taking in relationships. Flirts, negotiates, asserts socially with easy grace.
- **Square:** Romantic and creative tension. Push-pull between peace/closeness and independence/action. Mastering creates electrifying creative drive and passionate partnerships.
- **Trine:** Natural social ease, artistic talent, balanced chemistry. Attracts romantic partners and creative opportunities without unnecessary conflict.
- **Opposition:** Dynamic romantic attraction and relationship polarity. See-saw between compromising for harmony and asserting independence — mastery is the art of healthy passionate partnership.

### Venus & Jupiter — connection & joy/abundance

- **Conjunction:** Generous, warm-hearted, joyful. Loves celebrating life, sharing resources, creating a fun, luxurious atmosphere.
- **Sextile:** Social ease and good fortune in partnerships. Collaborative efforts and friendships naturally bring mutually beneficial opportunities.
- **Square:** Taste for luxury or overindulgence that can strain budgets or boundaries. Moderating generosity ensures lasting satisfaction.
- **Trine:** Natural charisma, social popularity, easy appreciation for life's pleasures. Brings peace and goodwill wherever they go.
- **Opposition:** Extravagance or generous expectations in relationships. Balancing the desire to please and indulge with realistic financial and emotional boundaries creates joyful, sustainable connections.

### Venus & Saturn — connection & loyalty/commitment

- **Conjunction:** Deeply loyal, enduring, cautious in love. Values long-term commitment and financial stability far more than superficial flattery.
- **Sextile:** Practical relationship skills and sound financial judgment. Invests time and heart into reliable, trustworthy connections.
- **Square:** Early fears of rejection or feelings of relationship awkwardness. Working through this teaches profound self-worth; leads to unshakeable, mature partnerships.
- **Trine:** Natural fidelity, emotional maturity, steady values. Relationships improve with age; rest on solid foundations of mutual respect.
- **Opposition:** Confronting feelings of relationship duty, distance, or caution through others. Learning vulnerability and healthy boundaries co-exist creates deeply secure, loyal bonds.

### Mars & Jupiter — action & expansion/courage

- **Conjunction:** High physical energy, bold entrepreneurial drive, infectious enthusiasm. Takes daring initiatives; watch for reckless haste.
- **Sextile:** Confident enterprise. Spots good opportunities and has the energy and courage to act on them immediately.
- **Square:** Impulsive bursts of energy; over-commits to too many battles at once. Strategic focus turns fiery ambition into massive real-world impact.
- **Trine:** Natural leadership, optimism in action, great sportsmanship. Pursues big goals confidently; bounces back quickly from defeat.
- **Opposition:** Pushing too hard in pursuit of big beliefs, or encountering strong philosophical competitors. Harnessing the energy creates a principled champion who acts with integrity and strategic patience.

### Mars & Saturn — action & discipline/endurance

- **Conjunction:** Controlled power, immense focus, relentless work ethic (the "gas pedal and brake" in unison). Produces work that withstands time.
- **Sextile:** Strategic patience and practical stamina. Paces oneself to complete long, grueling projects without burnout.
- **Square:** Frustration when immediate action hits a brick wall of delay or rules. Learning timing is everything transforms tension into unbeatable discipline and resilience.
- **Trine:** Calm perseverance, excellent timing, steady endurance. Channels energy purposefully; achieves milestones through consistent, calm effort.
- **Opposition:** Oscillation between pushing aggressively forward and stopping out of caution. Mastering this see-saw teaches supreme tactical timing — when to strike and when to hold ground.

### Jupiter & Saturn — expansion & structure/reality

- **Conjunction:** The builder's balance — anchoring big visions into concrete, lasting institutions. Dreams big while keeping feet firmly on the ground.
- **Sextile:** Realistic optimism. Plans for steady, sustainable growth without falling prey to foolish speculation.
- **Square:** Recurring cycle between over-expanding and retreating into caution. Balancing faith with pragmatic budgeting creates durable, generational achievements.
- **Trine:** Wise, balanced judgment and steady growth. Combines philosophical vision with practical common sense; earns widespread trust.
- **Opposition:** Managing tension between taking leaps of faith and upholding structured caution. Learns to evaluate risks objectively — a trusted mentor and strategic planner.

## 5. Notes for Urania (private-facing)

**When to reach for this file:**

- Austin asks me to read for a friend, family member, or someone he is helping — the friends-and-family register named in SOUL.md.
- The subject has zero or beginner-level astrological literacy.
- I need a metaphor for the "so that means" line that will land without a jargon-glossary paragraph attached.

**When NOT to reach for this file:**

- Austin's own briefings, forecasts, natal reads, or solar returns. He wants the technical register with modal-mood interpretation. He is not a client.
- Client-register readings (as opposed to friend-register), where precision and citation are the point. Use tradition language there; borrow only the *delivery-framework* discipline from this file, not the metaphors.
- Any moment where the two-body-only frame would obscure what sect + dignity + house + timelord are actually doing. If the fuller Hellenistic read contradicts the flat archetypal read, name the fuller read.

**How to use it:**

1. Compute the chart. Establish the Hellenistic frame (sect, ASC, LoY, standout signatures).
2. Identify the two or three load-bearing aspects for the question.
3. For each: name the two roles in plain English, describe the dynamic with the aspect's metaphor from §1, and close with a concrete behavioral suggestion — the three delivery rules in §3.
4. House placement gets named in plain English inside the "located in your chart" clause, not in Hellenistic shorthand. *"Your seventh house, which is where partnerships and one-on-one commitments live for you."*

**Outer-planet aspects** (Uranus / Neptune / Pluto to inner bodies or to each other) are not tabulated in the source document at pair-by-pair depth. When one is load-bearing, I compose the reading from the archetype one-liners in §2 combined with the aspect metaphor in §1 — using the same three-part delivery.

**Retrograde, applying/separating, and orb** are not discussed in this document. In client-facing reads I do not surface those distinctions unless they change the practical takeaway; when they do, I explain them in one plain sentence.
