So the full window you should hold in mind is approximately September 15th through December 10th. The three sharpest moments inside that window are the retrograde station on October 3rd, the sign change back into Libra around October 19th, and the direct station on November 13th.

What Venus retrograde traditionally addresses.

Ancient sources on Venus retrograde are consistent on a few themes. Rhetorius and Valens both treat Venus retrograde as a revision period for the matters Venus governs. Not a shutdown. Not a disaster. A revision. Modern Hellenistic practice, following Chris Brennan and Demetra George, tends to describe Venus retrograde in three registers.

The relational register. Old relationships resurface. Former partners, former friends, former loves make contact. Sometimes literally. Sometimes as memory or as dream. Current relationships get examined. What is the actual shape of this bond, versus what I have been telling myself it is. Whether the answer is renewal or release depends on what the relationship actually is, not on what Venus is doing.

The valuation register. Venus governs what we value. During her retrograde, values themselves get reviewed. What am I actually paying for with my time. What am I claiming to want that I do not actually want. What am I refusing to name that I do want. Money and finances often show up here because money is a proxy for value, but the deeper question is about what the money is being spent on and why.

The aesthetic and creative register. What you have been making. What you have been wearing, decorating with, surrounding yourself with. What has become stale. What wants to be reworked. Artists frequently revise old work during Venus retrograde. Sometimes projects that were abandoned come back and ask to be finished.

For a fourth house profection year, all three of these registers land specifically on the fourth house material. So the questions become. What in the shape of your home wants to be revised. What in your ancestry is asking to be seen. What in your family relationships needs an honest look. What in the foundations you have been building is not actually stable and needs to be redone. What relationships from your past, especially relationships that touch home or family, may resurface. What you value about the private life you have made.

How to read the retrograde in your specific case.

Given your chart and given your practice, three things are worth naming.

First, this is a chthonic retrograde. Scorpio is the traditional chthonic sign. Venus is uncomfortable there. She is uncomfortable in the same way that a beautiful thing is uncomfortable in a room full of shadows. She is not being destroyed. She is being asked to see what is in the shadow. For a practitioner devoted to Hekate, this is unusually resonant material. Venus walking backward through Scorpio, then crossing back into Libra to recompose herself, is a small mythic movement that echoes the Persephone descent and the Aphrodite Ourania distinction between the earthly and the heavenly Venus. Ancient sources are full of Venus in Scorpio being read as the Venus who has gone underground to see what she has been avoiding. She does not stay there. She comes back. But she comes back changed.

Second, because the retrograde arc runs from your fifth house back into your fourth house and then forward again, the material that is likely to surface will move in that direction. Whatever begins as an issue of pleasure, creativity, generativity, children, or sexual life may reveal itself, on the retrograde walk backward, to actually be a fourth house issue. Something about home. Something about roots. Something about the family you came from or the household you have made. And then when Venus stations direct in Libra deep in your fourth house on November 13th, the issue will resolve on the foundational side, not on the pleasure side. So if something surfaces during the first two weeks of October that seems to be about creative work or a pleasure or a fifth house matter, follow it backward with an open eye. It may be pointing at something deeper.

Third, your Lord of the Year is doing what Lords of the Year do. She is running the year. What emerges during this retrograde is likely to be the defining material of your entire age 39 profection year. Not a small event. A defining event. Whatever it is. Modal register, of course. Traditionally read as. Favors. Inclines toward. I do not predict specific events. But I do want to name clearly that a Venus retrograde in your Venus year, especially one that runs from her weakest to her strongest condition and passes through the profected house, is not background weather. It is the year showing its hand.

Between October 3rd and November 13th, expect Venus material to arrive. Old contacts. Old feelings. Old questions about the shape of your life. Meet what arrives. Do not run from it. Do not fix it prematurely. The whole point of a retrograde is that the answer requires the full walk backward before it can be seen.

Between mid November and mid February, Venus is going to make a long series of supportive contacts to almost every load bearing point in your chart. This is the harvest. Whatever you learned during the retrograde becomes usable, and the sky supports what you build with it.
