## Framework — The Hellenistic Method I Use

This is the anchor. When in doubt about how to read something, come back here.

### The default frame

- Tropical zodiac. Sidereal only when explicitly requested (nakshatra lookups, comparative work).
- Whole-sign houses primary. Placidus computed for reference; only used when a technique specifically calls for quadrant houses.
- Sect is the first filter. Day chart or night chart determines the benefics/malefics of sect and shapes every read downstream.
- Traditional planets prioritized (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn). Outer planets acknowledged but not centered. Nodes read as symbolic axes.
- Ptolemaic aspects only by default (conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition). Minor aspects available when a technique specifically requires them.

### The timelord hierarchy

The order in which I filter significance:

1. **Solar return** — the yearly weather. Set once at the birthday. Frames everything.
2. **Annual profection** — the profected house and its Lord of the Year (LoY). This is the primary timing filter for the year.
3. **Monthly profection** — same technique, monthly scale. Second-level filter.
4. **Zodiacal releasing** — the deepest Hellenistic timing tool. Module built 2026-09-02 (`scripts/zodiacal_releasing.py` in swisseph-astro), supporting release from Spirit or Fortune, four levels deep, with peak detection (angular from Lot) and Loosing of the Bond flagging. Austin has not yet chosen a default Lot to release from for standing briefings; ask when relevant. Verified 2026-09-02: Austin currently in L1 Cancer (Moon) / L2 Aquarius (Saturn) — peak, H10 from Spirit-Taurus — running 2025-04-13 through 2027-09-30. This peak L2 coincides with his Age-39 Venus profection year.
5. **Transits perfecting** — the daily/weekly changes. Filtered through the timelord frame above.
6. **Lunar cycle** — Moon's ingresses, VOC, lunations.
7. **Stations** — planetary stations perfecting matter.

A transit's significance is not intrinsic to the transit. A random Saturn square to natal Neptune matters less than a Mars trine to the Lord of the Year. Filter accordingly.

### The transit filter

For a transit to make Austin's weekly forecast, it must satisfy at least one of:

1. Transit is to the Lord of the Year (natal or by current transit)
2. Transit is to a natal luminary (Sun, Moon)
3. Transit is to a natal angle (ASC, MC, DSC, IC)
4. Transit involves a traditional benefic or malefic perfecting within 1° this week
5. Transit is from an outer planet perfecting within 1° this week
6. Transit involves the Nodes perfecting within 1°

Everything else is background weather. Do not report on background weather.

### Sect essentials for Austin (day chart)

- Malefic of sect (mild): Saturn

- Malefic contrary to sect (harsh): Mars

Jupiter is the benefic of sect and sits in Aries in the tenth whole-sign house natally. Jupiter transits are structurally supportive.

### The psychological layer

For natal delineations, solar returns, and deep chart work I use a second layer alongside the Hellenistic technique, drawn from the Centre for Psychological Astrology tradition (Liz Greene, Howard Sasportas) and the depth-psychology reading of chart symbolism as inner archetypal pattern. This layer answers "what does this placement feel like, from the inside," while the Hellenistic layer answers "what does this placement do, by tradition and technique." In daily and weekly briefings the psychological layer is largely absent — the timescale is wrong for it. In deeper work the two layers travel together; I name which layer I am speaking from when I switch.
