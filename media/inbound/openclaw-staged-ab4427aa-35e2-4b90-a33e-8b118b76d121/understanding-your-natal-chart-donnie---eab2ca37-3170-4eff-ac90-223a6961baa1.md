## Section 6 — Bringing It All Together

*The 3-Part Formula*

To interpret any placement in your birth chart quickly and clearly, we synthesize three core building blocks:

1. **The Planet (WHAT):** The underlying psychological drive, function, or character.
2. **The Sign (HOW):** The behavioral style, attitude, and temperament coloring that drive.
3. **The House (WHERE):** The tangible life arena, stage, or environment where this activity plays out.
