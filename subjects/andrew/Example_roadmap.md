**The Planet + Sign + House formula.**

Every placement in your chart follows a three-part structure that, once you see it, unlocks the whole reading. Think of it this way:

- The **Planet** is the *Actor* — what part of you is showing up.
- The **Sign** is the *Costume* — the style and mode in which that part of you expresses itself.
- The **House** is the *Stage* — the arena of life where the action takes place.

Any single placement in your chart can be read by asking: *Who is acting? In what style? On what stage?*

## GLOSSARY_TERMS

- **Ascendant**: The zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of your birth. It represents your outer persona, physical vitality, and the way you meet the world before you say a word. It also sets the whole house structure of your chart.
- **Chart Ruler**: The planet that rules your Ascendant sign. Its condition — where it is placed by sign and house, and how well-dignified it is — describes how your whole life-project runs. It is the single most important placement in a Hellenistic delineation.
- **Domicile**: The condition of a planet being in the sign it rules. A domiciled planet operates at full strength, fluently and reliably, on its own terrain.
- **Exaltation**: The condition of a planet being not just at home but honored — operating at the top of its register, with unusual grace and generosity of scope. There is one sign of exaltation for each traditional planet.
- **Detriment**: The condition of a planet being in the sign opposite its domicile — working against the grain of the sign it is in. Not disastrous, but requires more effort to express the planet's function.
- **Fall**: The condition of a planet being in the sign opposite its exaltation — the most constrained condition short of detriment. Fallen planets tend to work through discipline and structure rather than easy expression.
- **Face**: A small essential dignity in traditional astrology — a 10° segment of a sign where a planet has a modest place of its own. It gives a planet the ability to hold its ground even when other conditions are difficult.
- **Sextile**: An aspect of 60° between two planets. A supportive, active aspect — the two planets can cooperate easily when their placements are engaged.
- **Stellium**: A concentration of three or more planets in a single sign or house. It pulls the chart's center of gravity toward that arena — wherever the stellium lands, the chart focuses.
- **North Node**: One of two points where the Moon's orbit crosses the ecliptic — the northern crossing. In traditional practice it is read as the direction of soul-growth for this lifetime, the developmental vector you are here to move toward.
